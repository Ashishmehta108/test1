How it works

A tiny invisible picture does the tracking

Every email gets its own invisible picture. When the person opens the email, their mail app loads the picture from our tracker, and that tells us they opened it.

Links work the same way. A link in your email goes through the tracker first, and the click is recorded before the person lands on your page.

Keeping it accurate

Not every picture load is a real person

Gmail, Apple and office security tools sometimes load pictures on their own. The tracker checks every load against these questions before counting it.
